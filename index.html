The story so far

Food production and wildlife aren't in competition. Done right, each makes the other thrive.

In 2021 I co-founded Roots Allotments and, as one of three co-CEOs from 2021 to 2026, grew it into a national community growing network: 20 communities, 5,000 allotment patches and over 250 acres opened up for people to grow their own food.

My side of the business was the member experience end to end. I managed the Patch Manager team across every site and worked hand in hand with operations to keep sites running smoothly and members' experiences superb. I oversaw seeds, plants and growing concept for the network, and shaped the full events schedule across our sites with the events team. I also led the biodiversity programme: 3,000+ trees planted, 2,500m+ of native hedgerow, ponds, and 47 acres of wildflower meadow.

As the brand face of Roots I fronted its content, developed the long-form content strategy, and represented the company in the media, on podcasts and on television, including BBC's Just One Thing (Series 2, Episode 3: Gardening, presented by Zoe Ball), inspiring people in Croydon to pick, taste and get their hands in the soil.

Alongside the growing work, I build brands. It's a craft I learned over seven years in advertising, at Vice Media and Saatchi & Saatchi, before setting out on my own. I've relaunched websites, set up CRMs, built custom booking systems and created content that helps good businesses reach the people who need them. Growing spaces, growing businesses: it turns out the same care applies to both.

Biodiverse, edible growing spaces

For developers, landowners, councils & communities

I design and deliver growing spaces that feed people and support wildlife: allotment and community growing sites, native hedgerows, ponds, wildflower areas and orchards, with the events and growing education that bring them to life.

For developers and landowners: new developments in England must deliver at least 10% biodiversity net gain. Edible growing spaces are one of the few ways to meet that requirement with something residents genuinely use and love. I design allotment and community growing spaces that contribute towards BNG, build community from day one, and make developments easier to sell. See the Q Homes case study.

Proof: 9 bespoke allotments, with hedgerow, ponds and wild areas, at the UK's most sustainable housing development.
